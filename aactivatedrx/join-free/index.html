Questions, answered

Is it really free?
Yes. The free membership costs $0, needs no card, and you can upgrade to Premium any time.
How do member rewards work?
Your portal gives you a Join link to share. When someone you personally enroll becomes an active, paid Premium member, you earn a monthly member reward: $15 a month as a Free member, or $30 a month as a Premium member. Rewards are paid only on active Premium memberships as described in the Member Agreement. Prescription products, consultations and labs never earn a reward.
Do I have to sell anything?
No. There is no inventory, no selling scripts and no minimums. You share your links, and we handle checkout, fulfillment, support and payouts.
Are the products legal?
Yes. A licensed U.S. provider evaluates you through telehealth. If treatment is right for you, a prescription is written in your name and a state licensed 503A pharmacy prepares it for you. That is the difference from sites that sell vials labeled “research use only, not for human consumption.”
