Questions, answered

When will I notice a difference?
Everyone is different. Skin changes take consistent use over weeks, not days.
What should I expect with tretinoin?
Tretinoin can cause dryness, redness, peeling and sun sensitivity, especially in the first weeks, so daily sunscreen matters. It is not for use during pregnancy or while trying to conceive. Your provider reviews your history and explains how to use it.
Can men use this serum?
Yes. It is prescribed for women and men who want to support skin health. Your provider confirms whether it is right for you.
Do I need a prescription?
Tretinoin + GHK-Cu Serum is a prescription product, but you do not need one before you start. Your online visit is how a licensed provider decides whether it is right for you.
Is this legal?
Yes. A licensed U.S. provider evaluates you through telehealth. If treatment is right for you, a prescription is written in your name and a state licensed 503A pharmacy prepares it for you. That is the difference from sites that sell vials labeled “research use only, not for human consumption.”
